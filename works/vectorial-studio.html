A bold transformation for a global creative studio

Overview
As part of a branding competition, this rebrand for Vectorial Studio, a global design agency with roots in Peru, Qatar, and Algeria was a bold creative challenge. The task was to craft a fresh identity that reflects their global presence, modern aesthetic, and design authority. I approached it with purpose designing a system that’s refined, expressive, and built for impact.
